What you need to start

  • CPL with a valid or expired ME-IR
  • 70 hours pilot in command on aeroplanes
  • ATPL theory all subjects passed
  • aUPRT advanced upset recovery training certificate. Don’t have it? We offer it.
  • MCC certificate, or combine it with your rating as integrated iMCC
  • ICAO English 4–6 or take our ELP exam online
  • Medical Class 1 valid for the simulator test and base training. Not included in the course price.
Why a type rating makes you employable

Airlines prefer “ready entry” pilots: type rated, no wait for licence endorsement, no bonding. A type rating also means higher flexibility across airlines and an easy path to widebodies.

Airline placement support

Yes. Our Gold package adds CV and interview coaching, simulator assessment prep and our airline application portal. See packages.

Rules to know (EASA FCL)
  • Theory and simulator phases must finish within 6 months.
  • Landing training must follow within 6 months of the LST, or the rating starts again.
  • Privileges are as per the Type Certificate and Airbus Difference Requirements tables.

LPC & Revalidation

Keep your type rating valid.

Book at least 90 days before expiry and you extend your rating by 12 months. The check covers all mandatory EASA exercises, such as engine failure after V1, rejected take-offs and single-engine go-arounds.

SFI & TRI Instructor

Teach the next generation.

Become an A320 or B737 airline instructor and work freelance or employed, worldwide. Courses from EUR 4,995.

TRI: Type Rating Instructor

Turns experienced airline pilots into educators: teaching techniques, simulator operation and type-specific instruction. With extra assessments you can conduct base training and line flying under supervision.

SFI: Synthetic Flight Instructor

Instruction in the simulator, with teaching skills and session management. A320 and B737.

Prerequisites (EASA Part-FCL)

TRI (MPA): valid CPL, MPL or ATPL and type rating; teaching and learning course; 1,500 hours on multi-pilot aeroplanes; 30 route sectors in the past 12 months (15 may be in an FFS).

SFI (A): CPL, MPL or ATPL held; teaching and learning course; type proficiency check in an FFS within 12 months; 1,500 hours on multi-pilot aeroplanes; recent route sectors or line-oriented simulator sessions. Always check the current FCL revision.

MCF & Demo Pilot

Maintenance check flights.

EASA SPO requires a course before maintenance check flights (Level A) and demo flights. Types: A320, A330/350, B737, ATR and Embraer E170–195. Our instructors have extensive demo and test-flight experience, including lessee changes and returns to lessor.

  • Content SPO.SPEC.MCF regulation, risk management, flight profiles, ATC coordination, reports.
  • Format Theory plus Level D simulator. On request from two participants.
  • For type-rated pilots (PIC: 1,000 h on similar aircraft) and task specialists.
