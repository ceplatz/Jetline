About Jetline

Instructors and examiners. Worldwide.

A group of experienced instructors and examiners specialised in type ratings, instructor training and initial experience support, mainly on the A320 and B737.

AboutTeamLocationsApprovals
Mission & Vision

Passionate about forming airline pilots.

We take pilots from ab-initio to a frozen ATPL, through a type rating and on to airline selection, with the highest training standards.

Mission

Dedicated, experienced instructors. Airline assessment preparation after the course. Simulators worldwide, to airline standard wherever it suits you.

Vision

After-training support, job-search help and assessment consulting. Airline line training availability for every student. Affordable training for the next pilot generation.
