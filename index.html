Ready to start?

What you need to begin.

For EASA flight crew licence holders, these documents go to the flight school on enrolment, and at the latest before simulator training.

  • CPL with a valid or expired ME-IR rating
  • 70 hours of pilot-in-command time on aeroplanes
  • ATPL theory exams passed, all subjects
  • aUPRT advanced upset recovery training certificate
  • MCC certificate, or combine MCC with your type rating as integrated iMCC
  • ICAO English level 4–6 and a valid Class 1 medical
After the rating

Job-ready on day one.

Airlines prefer candidates who are already type rated, with no waiting for licence endorsement. Jetline backs that up with career support built around the Airline Basic Qualification (BQ).

  • Airline assessment and psychometric test preparation
  • Interview training, CV and cover letter coaching
  • Simulator assessment preparation and A320 technical refreshers
  • A sim check after one year in selected packages (Gold)
